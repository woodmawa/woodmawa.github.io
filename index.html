About Softwood Consulting

Softwood Consulting specializes in building elegant, maintainable open source solutions using Groovy and modern JVM technologies. We believe in the power of open source software to solve real-world problems while fostering collaboration and innovation.

📚 Documentation & Support

Comprehensive documentation, examples, and community support for all our projects.

Every project we create is designed with clarity, maintainability, and real-world usability in mind. We focus on solving genuine problems that developers face, backed by solid engineering principles and thorough testing.
